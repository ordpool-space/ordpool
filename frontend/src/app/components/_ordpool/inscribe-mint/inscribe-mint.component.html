Inscribe a file on Bitcoin

Upload any file (image, SVG, text, JSON, PDF) and inscribe it directly onto Bitcoin from your own wallet. No service fee (you pay only the network miner fee), non-custodial (your wallet signs the transactions, we never touch your keys), and every inscription also mints two free CAT-21 cats 😺 as a side effect.
